# 🚀 Developer Portfolio Website

Welcome to your custom portfolio website! This project is designed to showcase your skills, software projects, work experience, and contact details to potential employers and recruiters.

---

## 📂 Project Files

- **`index.html`**: The main page content containing all sections (Hero, About, Skills, Projects, Experience, Contact).
- **`styles.css`**: Modern styling featuring Dark/Light mode theme switching, glassmorphism, responsive grid layouts, and animations.
- **`script.js`**: Interactive features including theme toggles, project filter tabs, mobile navigation, and contact form handling.

---

## 🛠️ Step 1: Preview & Customize Your Content

### How to Preview Locally:
Simply double-click `index.html` in your file explorer to open it in any web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari).

### Customization Checklist:
Open `index.html` in your text editor and replace the following placeholder items:
1. **Name & Title**: Search for `[Your Name]` and replace it with your actual name.
2. **Contact Links**:
   - Change `your.email@example.com` to your real email address.
   - Update `https://github.com` with your GitHub profile link.
   - Update `https://linkedin.com` with your LinkedIn profile link.
3. **Resume Download**: Update the `id="resume-link"` href attribute to point to your PDF resume (e.g. `resume.pdf`).
4. **Projects**: Edit the titles, descriptions, technology badges, and live demo / code links in the `<section id="projects">`.
5. **Experience**: Edit your previous roles, company names, and dates in `<section id="experience">`.

---

## 🌐 Step 2: Host Online for Free (Get Your Resume URL)

To put a live website address on your resume, deploy your site to one of these free hosting platforms:

### Option A: GitHub Pages (Recommended - Free)
1. Create a free account on [GitHub.com](https://github.com).
2. Create a new public repository named: `yourusername.github.io` (replace `yourusername` with your GitHub username).
3. Upload all three files (`index.html`, `styles.css`, `script.js`) into the repository.
4. Go to **Repository Settings** > **Pages** > Select `main` branch > Click **Save**.
5. Your website will be live at: `https://yourusername.github.io` within 1-2 minutes!

### Option B: Vercel / Netlify (Free Drag-and-Drop)
1. Create a free account on [Vercel.com](https://vercel.com) or [Netlify.com](https://netlify.com).
2. Drag and drop the `portfolio-website` folder directly into their deployment dashboard.
3. You will instantly get a custom link like `https://yourname.vercel.app` or `https://yourname.netlify.app`.

## 📝 Step 3: Put the Link on Your Resume

Add your new portfolio URL to the header section of your resume alongside your contact details:

```text
John Doe
Full-Stack Software Developer
Location: New York, NY | Phone: (123) 456-7890
Email: john.doe@email.com | Portfolio: https://johndoe.github.io | LinkedIn: linkedin.com/in/johndoe
```

> 💡 **Pro Tip**: In Word or Canva, make sure the text `https://johndoe.github.io` is a clickable hyperlink so employers can click it directly from your PDF resume!
